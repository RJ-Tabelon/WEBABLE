City council approves new bus routes

By Staff Writer · 4 min read

The city council voted on Tuesday to add three new bus routes connecting the east side to downtown. Officials said the routes will start running in the spring and will run every fifteen minutes during peak hours.

Residents at the meeting raised concerns about stop placement near schools. The transit office said it would publish a map for public comment next month.
